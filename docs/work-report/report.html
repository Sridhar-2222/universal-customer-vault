4. The Vault

The Vault is one shared part for both apps. It keeps a business's GST accounts and bill photos, and each customer's own documents.

AreaWhat it does
Customer documentsBills, warranties, bookings and papers. A business can send documents to a customer only after the customer agrees with the business's 8-letter code. Sharing can have an end date and be taken back.
Business accountsSale invoices and purchase bills with GST checks (valid GSTIN, CGST = SGST, IGST vs same state, totals add up, duplicate numbers), payments, dues and overdue, cash and bank books, profit and loss, balance sheet, GST summary.
Personal accountsIncome and expenses for a person, kept separate from any business.
Bill photosKept in the business's own private folder; members only; cannot be deleted because they are proof for the books.
ApprovalStaff can enter bills; only the owner or an admin can post them. Bills read from photos need a tick: "I checked these details against the paper bill".
Link to Fingertip shopsEach shop is its own Vault business. Fingertip's owner, admin and staff roles apply; removing someone in Fingertip ends their Vault access at once. The owner can add the shop's GSTIN.
Not in the VaultHealth records. Patient files stay in Rx Fingertip's stricter health store.

5. Reading bills from photos (OCR)

Tested on 27 sample GST bills damaged like phone photos: every amount, tax, total, GSTIN and date was read correctly, and 89% of bills were right in every field.

Kind of photoResult
Clean, tilted, sideways, upside down, shadowed, phone-quality, PDFAll fields right
BlurredAmounts right; words in names run together (a warning is shown)
SpeedAbout 4–5 seconds per photo; PDFs instantly
  • Engines: PaddleOCR's reading models first, Tesseract as backup.
  • Fixes made while testing: "lnvoice No" (a small L read for I) now found; upside-down bills fixed (0% to 100%); software versions locked so a new install reads the same way.
  • Not yet tested on real paper bills; handwriting is only flagged; the reading service still has to be put online.

6. Safety

The database itself decides who sees what, so a mistake in the app cannot show one shop's data to another.

  • Each person sees only their own documents; a business sees only its own records and what customers chose to share.
  • Nobody writes to tables directly; every change goes through checked steps that confirm who you are and your role.
  • Files are private and open only through links that expire within minutes.
  • Accounts must balance; a posted entry cannot be edited, only reversed.
  • Every action on a document is written to a history that nobody can change.
  • Tested against unsafe copies of Fingertip's names: the Vault uses only its own checks.
  • Supabase's security advisor shows no warnings for the Vault.

7. Sign-in problem and the fixes

Sign-in codes stop arriving because Supabase's built-in email sender allows only a few emails per hour for the whole app. The logs show "email rate limit exceeded".

Message in the logsMeaning
email rate limit exceededMain problem: codes are not sent once the hourly limit is used
